ERASMUS+

Discover Europe Through Learning

Erasmus+ opens doors to international experiences, new skills, friendships and personal growth for young people.

WHAT IS ERASMUS+?

Europe’s Programme for Education, Training, Youth and Sport

Erasmus+ is the European Union’s programme that supports education, training, youth and sport in Europe. It offers opportunities for young people to study, train, gain experience and volunteer abroad.

Through non-formal learning, youth exchanges and volunteering, participants develop skills, intercultural understanding and a stronger sense of European citizenship.

OPPORTUNITIES

What Can You Join?

Different types of activities for different goals and age groups.

Youth Exchanges

Short-term group projects where young people from different countries meet, live and learn together.

  • • Age: usually 13–30
  • • Duration: 5–21 days
  • • Themes: culture, environment, inclusion...

Training Courses

Capacity-building activities for youth workers and young leaders focused on specific skills.

  • • For youth workers & leaders
  • • Duration: usually 5–10 days
  • • Topics: digital, inclusion, project management...

European Solidarity Corps (ESC)

Volunteering opportunities that allow young people to support communities across Europe.

  • • Age: 18–30
  • • Short-term or long-term
  • • Fully funded in most cases

Other Activities

Job shadowing, study visits, partnership building and more opportunities under Erasmus+.

  • • Professional development
  • • Networking
  • • Project partnerships
